## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an **India-focused sports footwear e-commerce sales analysis** created using Microsoft Excel.

The project started with a publicly available sports footwear sales dataset and was **cleaned, transformed and adapted into an India-focused business scenario** for analytical and portfolio purposes.

The final output is an interactive Excel dashboard designed to provide a quick overview of sales performance, customer behavior and product/brand performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer key business questions such as:

- How is revenue performing over the years?
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which brands perform the best?
- Which Indian states contribute the most revenue?
- What is the contribution of different customer types?
- How do different sales channels perform?
- What are the overall business KPIs?

## 📈 Dashboard Components

### Revenue Trend

Tracks yearly revenue performance from **2018 to 2025** and highlights changes in overall sales performance.

### Top 5 Products

Identifies the five products generating the highest revenue.

### Top 5 States

Highlights the top-performing Indian states based on revenue contribution.

### Revenue by Brand

Compares revenue performance across major footwear brands.

### Revenue by Customer Type

Shows the revenue contribution from different customer segments.

### Interactive Slicers

The dashboard includes interactive filters for:

- Year
- Brand
- Category
- Sales Channel

Changing a slicer dynamically updates the dashboard KPIs and visualizations.

## 🔄 Data Transformation

The original dataset was used as the **base/source dataset** and was subsequently modified for this project.

The transformation process included:

- Cleaning the original dataset
- Restructuring fields for analysis
- Adapting the dataset to an Indian business scenario
- Modifying geographical information to represent Indian states
- Preparing customer and sales attributes
- Creating analysis-ready calculated fields
- Structuring the dataset for PivotTables and PivotCharts

> **Note:** The resulting dataset is a transformed analytical dataset created for portfolio and learning purposes. It does not represent actual Indian market sales data.
